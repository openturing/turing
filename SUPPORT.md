## 5. What is *not* free

To keep expectations clear:

- ❌ Guaranteed response times → **Business/Enterprise support**
- ❌ Someone deploying it for you → **Installation & setup**
- ❌ A connector/feature built to your spec → **Custom development**
- ❌ Use in a closed product without Apache 2.0 obligations → **Commercial license**

Everything else — the full platform, all connectors, self-hosting at any
scale — is and stays free.
