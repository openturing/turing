Delegate a focused sub-task to a specialist agent — a colleague with its own expertise, tools and skills — and use its answer to help the user. Call this when the request (or part of it) is squarely in another specialist's domain rather than your own. Parameters: `agent` is the specialist's name, exactly as listed in your available specialists; `task` is a clear, self-contained description of what you need that specialist to do (include any context it needs, since it does not see this conversation). The specialist runs autonomously and returns its result as text; weave that result into your reply to the user, and you may delegate to more than one specialist if needed. Only the specialists listed for you are reachable.
