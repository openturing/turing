Build Mermaid `mindmap` source from a body of text around a central term. Pass the source `text` (e.g. concatenated abstracts) and the central `term`. Returns a Mermaid mindmap whose root is the term and whose branches are the most frequent meaningful words in the text (stop-words and the term itself removed), title-cased. Use to turn a set of sources into a visual topic map before planning or summarizing.
