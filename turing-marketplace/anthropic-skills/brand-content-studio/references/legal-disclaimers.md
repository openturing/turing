# Legal Disclaimers

Mandatory disclaimers by content category. In the compliance pass (step 7),
identify every category the draft touches and append the matching block
**verbatim** at the end of the piece, under a `---` separator.

A draft that touches a category below without its disclaimer is **not
publishable**.

## finance

Triggered by: any mention of payments, returns, rates, fees, investment, or a
forward-looking monetary number.

```
This content is for informational purposes only and is not financial advice.
Figures are estimates and may change. Acme Pay is a registered service of Acme
Corp. Terms apply; see acme.example/terms.
```

## health

Triggered by: any health, wellness, or medical claim.

```
This content is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before
acting on any information here.
```

## product-claims

Triggered by: a comparative or superlative claim about an Acme product
("faster", "cheaper", "more secure").

```
Product claims are based on internal testing as of the publication date and may
vary by configuration. See acme.example/methodology for details.
```

## general

Always appended to externally published content (in addition to any of the
above):

```
© Acme Corp. All rights reserved. The Acme name and logo are trademarks of Acme
Corp.
```

## Ordering

When multiple categories apply, append in this order: finance, health,
product-claims, general.
