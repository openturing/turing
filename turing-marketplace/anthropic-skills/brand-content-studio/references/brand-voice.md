# Acme Brand Voice

The complete voice ruleset. The `SKILL.md` carries only a summary; this file is
the authoritative reference, loaded on demand.

## The beat model

Every piece of narrative content is a sequence of **beats**. A beat is:

- a short, descriptive title (2–5 words), and
- one self-contained paragraph of body.

Rules:

1. The first beat is always a **Hook** — a concrete, specific opening that
   earns the next sentence. Never open with throat-clearing ("In today's
   fast-paced world…").
2. The last beat is always a **Call to Action** — one unambiguous next step.
3. Body beats develop a single idea each, and must be **reorderable** without
   breaking the piece (no "as we saw above").

## Tone

- Confident, concrete, jargon-free.
- Active voice. ("Acme Pay schedules the payment" — not "the payment is
  scheduled by Acme Pay".)
- Short sentences over long ones. One idea per sentence.
- Address the reader as "you".

## Vocabulary

**Forbidden (never use):**

| Word/phrase | Use instead |
|---|---|
| synergy | "works together" |
| disruptive | name the concrete change |
| world-class / best-in-class | a measurable claim, or nothing |
| leverage (verb) | "use" |
| seamless | describe what the user doesn't have to do |
| revolutionary | the specific improvement |

**Mandatory:**

- For product content, name the product in the **Hook beat**.
- For finance content, the word "estimate" or "may" near any forward-looking
  number (see `legal-disclaimers.md`).

## Cargo-register guidance

Shift register to the reader's seniority. Detect from the brief's `cargoHint`:

- **STRATEGIC** (C-level): lead with outcome and risk; one number that matters;
  no how-to detail. ~30% shorter.
- **MANAGERIAL** (directors/managers): outcome + the mechanism that delivers it;
  one concrete example.
- **FOUNDATIONAL** (analysts/ICs): step-by-step; show the mechanism in full;
  define terms on first use.

## Before / after

**Before:** "Acme leverages a world-class, seamless platform to disrupt
payments."

**After (MANAGERIAL):** "Acme Pay schedules a payment for the date you choose,
so a missed due date never costs you a late fee. You set it once; the money
moves on time."
