## FAQ

**Do I need this to use Turing in my company?**
No. Internal use and self-hosting are fully covered by Apache 2.0.

**Do I need this to offer Turing as a paid hosted service?**
Not strictly — Apache 2.0 permits it. A commercial license adds warranty,
indemnification, and the right to drop attribution in a closed UI.

**Can I get support without a commercial license?**
Yes — see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Support and licensing are sold separately.
